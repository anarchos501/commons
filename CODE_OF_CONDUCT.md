# Code of Conduct

Commons is built for mutual aid communities, so the project space should reflect care, consent, and practical respect.

Participants are expected to:

- Treat people with dignity.
- Assume good faith while still taking harm seriously.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, coercion, or demeaning behavior.
- Respect privacy and do not expose personal or sensitive community information.
- Make disagreements concrete, reviewable, and oriented toward repair.

Project maintainers may moderate issues, pull requests, discussions, and access when behavior undermines safety or participation.
