# Discussion

Discussion is the conversational layer of a Coordination Space.

It supports temporary coordination, exploration, and day-to-day communication. It is not a historical record system.

```text
Conversation is temporary.
Records are intentional.
```

## Boundaries

Commons does not provide unrestricted person-to-person private messaging. Discussion is attached to a group, project, responsibility, or later a specific coordination object because coordination is happening there.

Discussion is distinct from:

- Bulletins: announcements and reports.
- Publications: preserved entries and periodicals.
- Living Documents: current governing or reference texts.

When information should be preserved, members should intentionally move it into one of those record systems through the appropriate process.

## Threads And Messages

Every coordination space may have a `General Discussion` thread. Members may create additional threads without petition.

Only active members may create threads, post messages, or support a thread-closure petition. Quiet and dormant members may view discussion according to normal space visibility rules, but do not participate in active discussion.

Discussion does not include reactions, rankings, popularity metrics, or scoring.

## Retention

Discussion messages expire and are hard-deleted. Inactive threads expire and are hard-deleted. Discussion is not archived or hidden as historical content.

Governance may tune retention within bounded windows. Governance may not disable message expiration or thread expiration entirely.

## Closure

Thread creation is lightweight. Thread closure is petition-backed.

A closure petition validates that the thread belongs to the target coordination space. A thread is not owned by its creator once it is part of the space.

Concern action proposals may include thread closure as a future remedy, but concern review and thread closure remain distinct processes.
