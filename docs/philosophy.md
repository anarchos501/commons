# Philosophy

Commons exists to help communities coordinate mutual aid without turning coordination into surveillance, hierarchy, or permanent records of need.

The central design principle is: make cooperation easier and domination harder.

Commons should support self-organization, preserve dignity and consent, minimize vulnerability records, keep governance understandable, and allow groups to experiment, fork, migrate, and reverse decisions.

The deeper product distinction is that shared coordination should be visible and accountable, while personal life should remain private and sovereign.

Commons is infrastructure for self-organization, not a machine for controlling communities. It should provide memory, coordination, visibility, consent systems, accountability systems, privacy systems, and exit paths without trying to model all human relationships or replace human judgment.

The full project compass lives in the [Commons Charter](charter.md).
